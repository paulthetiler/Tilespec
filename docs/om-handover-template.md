## 4. Cleaning and maintenance — tailor to the installed finish
- Remove grit routinely with non-abrasive sweeping or vacuuming; use manufacturer-approved cleaning methods.
- For routine cleaning, use a compatible neutral cleaner at the maker's recommended dilution, followed by clean-water rinsing where instructed. Avoid residue-forming products.
- Do not assume acidic builders' cleaners are suitable. Check tile, grout, sealant, natural stone, polished finishes and any protective treatment first; obtain written manufacturer approval before acid, bleach, descaler or aggressive degreaser.
- Mechanical scrubbers, brushes, pressure washers and steam cleaners must be approved for the specific installation. Protect grout joints and movement joints from damage.
- Remove spills promptly, using a product-compatible method. Test unfamiliar cleaners in an inconspicuous area and follow the current product guidance.
- Wet areas: follow the specified cleaning and hygiene regime, check sealants and movement joints periodically and report failed joints or water ingress promptly.

## 5. Inspections, defects and exclusions
- Inspect movement joints, silicone, grout, cracked or loose tiles, water ingress and impact damage at a frequency suited to the premises and use.
- Do not cut, drill or fix into waterproofed areas without reviewing as-built records and obtaining appropriate advice.
- Report defects with location, photographs, date and relevant use/cleaning history; record the notified contact: [ ].
- State contract-specific warranty and defects liability provisions only after checking signed contract and manufacturers' conditions. Do not promise a universal warranty.
- Maintenance by others and damage from misuse or incompatible cleaners must be dealt with under the agreed contract terms.
