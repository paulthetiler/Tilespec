## 4. Sequence of work
1. Attend site induction; review current drawings, specifications, RAMS, permits, interfaces and work area readiness.
2. Inspect substrate for suitability, strength, movement, levels, contamination, moisture and any required surveys. Record concerns and obtain instruction before proceeding.
3. Coordinate deliveries and unload using suitable mechanical aids and competent operators. Secure materials and keep routes clear.
4. Protect adjacent finishes where included in TileSpec's scope; establish exclusion zones, dust controls and safe cutting/mixing stations.
5. Carry out agreed preparation: cleaning, repairs, primers, levelling, boarding, decoupling or waterproofing in the specified sequence. Observe product compatibility and cure requirements.
6. Set out from approved drawings, levels and datum lines; confirm movement joints, falls, edge details, interfaces and tile pattern.
7. Cut and handle tiles with appropriate wet cutting or effective extraction, RPE where required, lifting aids and guards.
8. Mix and apply specified adhesives in accordance with manufacturer instructions, substrate and tile requirements. Fix tiles with suitable coverage and alignment; verify workmanship as work progresses.
9. Allow specified curing periods before traffic, grouting or subsequent works. Grout and seal joints, form movement joints and silicone where specified, following product instructions.
10. Inspect and record quality, complete snagging, clean down and remove waste using approved arrangements.
11. Install, maintain or hand over finished-work protection according to the agreed scope; record handover and outstanding items.

**Important:** The old CTI method described cement screed, mallet fixing, 2–4 mm joints and a fixed 48-hour exclusion period for a particular 2015 job. Those are NOT universal TileSpec methods or curing requirements. Actual specification and manufacturer guidance govern.

## 5. Controls during work
Follow the project risk assessment, COSHH assessments and permits. Prevent uncontrolled silica dust; use safe lifting and access methods; coordinate with other trades; manage noise, vibration, electrical leads, slurry and sharp waste. Maintain housekeeping and stop/reassess if site conditions change.
