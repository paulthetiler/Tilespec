# TileSpec — COSHH Register (Working Template)
Version: 9 November 2026 | Internal draft | Product-specific verification required

## Purpose and controls
This register tracks substances used or generated by TileSpec activities. It is NOT a set of completed COSHH assessments. Before use, a competent person must obtain the current manufacturer's safety data sheet (SDS), assess the actual task, exposure route and workers, record controls, brief operatives and make relevant information accessible on site. An SDS alone is not a COSHH assessment. Review on substitution, changed process, new SDS or exposure incident.
