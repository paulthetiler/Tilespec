# TileSpec — Commercial Terms & Conditions (DRAFT FOR REVIEW)
Version: 9 November 2026. Internal working draft only. Not issued to customers or incorporated into contracts. Obtain UK construction-law review before use.

## 1. Scope and quotations
TileSpec offers commercial tiling packages covering agreed supply, substrate preparation, installation and supervision. The written quotation, specifications, drawings, exclusions and programme define the scope. Quotations remain valid for 30 days unless stated otherwise. Changes to quantities, specifications or programme may require repricing.

## 2. Payment
TileSpec's standard commercial position is a maximum 30 calendar day payment period, with clearly agreed payment applications, due dates, final dates for payment and statutory notices compliant with applicable construction payment law. Project-specific stage payments or fortnightly applications are to be agreed in writing. Longer terms require express approval before contract acceptance. A payment timetable must identify the application date, due date, final date for payment, required supporting evidence, payment notice and pay-less notice process. An invoice or application must not be rejected on unclear administrative grounds. The project must pass a documented cash-flow and counterparty credit review before acceptance.

## 3. Retention
TileSpec's opening position is no cash retention. Any retention must be expressly approved after a cash-flow and collection-risk review; 5% is an exceptional ceiling, not an automatic allowance. Proposed release: 2.5% of subcontract value at practical completion of TileSpec's subcontract works; remaining 2.5% after the agreed defects rectification period, normally 12 months, subject to contractual rectification obligations. Release should relate to TileSpec's works rather than the overall development where negotiable. Release dates, precise claim requirements and sums to be tracked and pursued; any unspecified or conditional release date is to be resolved before signing. Any alternative terms require approval.

## 4. Materials and procurement
Significant or bespoke tile orders require an agreed advance payment, procurement milestone or other suitable funding/security arrangement before TileSpec commits to purchasing. Any advance payment, title, vesting, storage, insurance and treatment on termination must be expressly agreed. Supplier lead times and substitutions require confirmation. Changes after order may incur unrecoverable committed costs.

## 5. Variations
Variations to scope, design, quantities, substrate condition or sequence must be instructed by an authorised representative and priced/approved in writing where practicable. Urgent instructed work should be promptly documented, with the contractual valuation and notice procedures followed. No assumption that a signed record alone confirms an agreed price.

## 6. Dayworks
Dayworks must be instructed and recorded daily, showing operatives, hours, plant, materials and nature of work, with site representative verification or documented refusal/unavailability. Charge-out rates, minimum periods, overtime, travel and plant/material charging are set out in the separate agreed Daywork Rate Schedule. Rates are provisional until approved for each contract.

## 7. Access, readiness and site facilities
Tender assumes suitable access, available working areas, safe substrates, and preceding trades complete as programmed. Contract documents should allocate responsibility for welfare, water, power, unloading, secure storage, skips, lifting and access equipment. Unexpected conditions or lack of access to be notified promptly.

## 8. Programme, disruption and delay
Pricing assumes agreed working hours, sequencing and continuity. Changes, standing time, remobilisation, repeated visits, out-of-hours working, acceleration or extended supervision may affect price and completion date, subject to the subcontract's notice and entitlement provisions. TileSpec will keep contemporaneous records and give required notices.

## 9. Quality, defects and damage
TileSpec will perform its agreed works with reasonable skill and care and rectify defects attributable to its work in accordance with the contract. Responsibility for unsuitable existing substrates, design by others, client-supplied materials and damage by other trades must be clearly allocated, without attempting to exclude non-excludable legal obligations. Snagging and defects procedures to be agreed.

## 10. Cancellation and suspension
Where work is cancelled, suspended or materially delayed, entitlement to payment for completed work, committed materials and reasonable proven additional costs will follow the agreed contract and applicable law. Any right to suspend for non-payment must comply with statutory and contractual requirements.

## 11. Insurance and safety
Insurance levels, RAMS, inductions, site rules, CDM responsibilities and any specialist training/accreditation must be agreed for each project.

## 12. Contract precedence and acceptance
These are proposed commercial terms only. They do not automatically override a main contractor's subcontract. Conflicting terms must be identified and agreed before signing. Do not accept unseen main-contract terms, unspecified schedules, deemed knowledge of documents not supplied, or retrospective incorporation of terms covering work already done. The signed document hierarchy, incorporated specifications and agreed departures must be recorded. No work or material commitments should proceed without appropriate authorisation and an agreed contractual basis.

## 13. Protection of completed works
Where protection of completed tiling is expressly included in TileSpec's agreed scope, TileSpec will supply and install the specified protection and fulfil any agreed maintenance or removal obligations. Where excluded, the main contractor or other designated party must arrange suitable protection and its maintenance. The tender/order should explicitly identify who supplies, installs, maintains, inspects and removes protection, and when responsibility transfers. Following documented handover, ongoing protection and prevention of damage by subsequent trades rests with the contractually responsible party. Third-party damage or interference with protection is not automatically a TileSpec workmanship defect; rectification may be chargeable subject to contract and evidence.

## 14. Coordination with other trades
The parties will coordinate access and working areas to allow safe, reasonably continuous installation. TileSpec will promptly notify interference, congestion or unavailability of areas and keep records of any resulting delay or additional costs. Any entitlement to time or money follows the agreed subcontract and notice requirements.

## 15. Authorised instructions and records
Before starting, the parties should identify the individuals authorised to instruct changes and approve commercial consequences. Site instructions, variations and dayworks should be confirmed in writing as soon as practicable. A signature verifying attendance or hours does not necessarily constitute agreement to valuation. Urgent safety-related instructions must be followed appropriately.

## 16. Substrate checks and concealed conditions
TileSpec will carry out the checks expressly included in its scope and notify apparent unsuitable substrates, moisture, movement, levels or hidden defects discovered during the works. Additional investigation, preparation, corrective work and associated programme effects are to be agreed and valued under the contract before proceeding where reasonably practicable. TileSpec must not knowingly proceed with unsafe or unsuitable work.

## 17. Design and specification responsibility
Design responsibility is limited to design expressly allocated to TileSpec in the accepted subcontract. Responsibility for designs and specifications prepared by others remains as allocated by contract and law; TileSpec will promptly raise apparent discrepancies or unsuitable specifications. No clause removes any statutory duty or expressly accepted design obligation.

## 18. Snagging and return visits
TileSpec will attend to genuine defects attributable to its works as required by the agreed contract. Return visits for third-party damage, changed instructions, unavailable access or issues outside TileSpec's responsibility may be separately valued where contractually permitted. Snag lists should identify location, nature and responsible trade, with reasonable access arranged.

## 19. Cooperation, communication and dispute escalation
TileSpec will work professionally with site personnel, communicate issues promptly and seek to deliver agreed quality and programmes. In return, the contractor is expected to provide timely instructions, coordination, reasonable access and payment in accordance with agreed terms. Concerns should first be raised with the nominated site and commercial contacts, then escalated to management promptly, without restricting statutory adjudication, payment or other legal rights.

## 20. Liability and subcontract review
Before acceptance, the parties should expressly review liability caps, indemnities, consequential-loss provisions, design obligations, warranties, delay damages and any flow-down obligations. Before signing, require express review of any transfer of design responsibility for work by others, pass-through main-contract delay damages, uncapped indemnities, unusually long limitation periods, unilateral set-off rights and termination provisions permitting prolonged withholding of earned sums. TileSpec does not agree to unlimited or uninsurable liabilities merely by issuing this draft; the signed subcontract determines obligations subject to applicable law.

## 21. Payment protection and counterparty risk
Before committing labour or significant procurement, TileSpec will assess the contractor's legal entity, trading and payment history, credit exposure and project funding arrangements. Where proportionate, request trade references, recent accounts or suitable payment security. Do not assume a well-known end client guarantees the main contractor's solvency. Agreed valuations, prompt written applications and an aged-debt and retention register are required. Any insolvency-related payment clause, suspension provision, restrictions on invoice financing or disputed withholding mechanism must be reviewed before acceptance. No clause in these proposed terms is a guarantee of recovery if a contractor becomes insolvent.

## 22. Delays, notices and programme changes
Tender allowances must identify access, sequence, working hours and expected continuity. Before signing, review notice deadlines (including very short two-day periods), loss-of-expense evidence requirements, exclusions for disruption by other trades, acceleration obligations and any right to revise the programme without compensation. TileSpec will issue contemporaneous notices and maintain records of labour, standing time, instructions and disrupted areas. Any contractual entitlement remains subject to the agreed subcontract and applicable law.

## 23. Final account, deductions and termination
Before signing, confirm final-account submission deadlines, documents required, when assessments become binding, retention claim procedures and the grounds and notice requirements for deductions. Challenge terms that permit withholding all payments for unrelated paperwork, collateral warranties or termination disputes without a proportionate and lawful basis. The treatment of work completed, off-site or on-site materials, demobilisation, equipment and any completion costs on termination must be agreed and documented.
